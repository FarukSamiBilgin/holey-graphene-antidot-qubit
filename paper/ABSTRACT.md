# Extended abstract

**Status:** Manuscript in preparation (unpublished).

A missing antidot in a graphene antidot lattice binds a localized in-gap state proposed as a spin-qubit host (Pedersen et al. 2008), while edge/midgap local moments have been identified as a competing dephasing source (Vanević et al. 2009). We tested, in one geometry scan, whether qubit quality q (isolation × defect weight) and parasitic edge moment p (mean |m| on rim atoms from collinear mean-field Hubbard) overlap or trade off, using periodic commensurate supercells (t = 2.7 eV, U/t = 1.15). A pre-registered rule, fixed before the data were seen, required the ordering (|dN| up ⇒ q up and p up) to hold under both Γ and 2×2 k-sampling and for supercell sizes Nd = 3 and 4. At Nd = 3 the ordering holds (dq = +0.079 at Γ, +0.118 at 2×2; dp = +0.066); at Nd = 4 it flips (dq = −0.091 at Γ, −0.095 at 2×2), the zigzag geometry becomes gapless and the armchair defect level disperses. By the pre-registered criterion the apparent trade-off is an ARTIFACT of the finite cell / Γ-point choice; the method at this scale does not establish the effect.

Note: an independent re-run with newer library versions (2026-11-15) reproduces the ARTIFACT verdict, but with the flip only at Nd = 4, 2×2 (dq = −0.0931); several near-zero-mode entries are version-dependent (see README).
